public beta · iPhone

come grow with us: try pocket planter before launch.

pocket planter is a plant care app that tells you what each plant needs today, based on your weather and the rooms they live in. The beta is free, and your feedback shapes what we ship.

join the beta on TestFlight how it works

Free. For iPhone with iOS 15.1 or later, in the US.

what it is

a care plan for every plant, in your pocket.

Add your plants, tell us which room they live in, and pocket planter builds a daily list of what to water, mist and feed. Here is what you can try in the beta.

a daily care schedule

Today's list adapts to your local weather and to each room's light, so a plant by a sunny window gets different care than one in a dim hallway.

plant ID from a photo

Snap a plant and get its name and a care plan. You can also ask what is wrong with a sad looking leaf.

ask planter

A friendly AI chat that already knows your plants, your rooms and your weather. Ask anything, from yellow leaves to repotting.

a light meter

Use your iPhone camera to measure how much light a spot really gets, then pick the right plant for it.

how to join

two steps, about two minutes.

Betas on iPhone are delivered through TestFlight, Apple's official app for trying apps before they reach the App Store.

  1. 1

    install TestFlight from the App Store

    TestFlight is free and made by Apple. On your iPhone, open the App Store, search for TestFlight and tap get.

    get TestFlight in the App Store
  2. 2

    tap the invite link and install pocket planter

    Open this page on your iPhone and tap the button below. TestFlight opens, tap accept, then install. pocket planter lands on your home screen like any other app.

    join the beta on TestFlight

Beta builds expire after 90 days. Keep automatic updates turned on in TestFlight so you always have the newest version.

questions

good to know

is it free?

Yes. The beta is free, and there are no payments in the app.

what is TestFlight?

TestFlight is Apple's official app for testing apps before they are released on the App Store. It is free, and it is how every iPhone beta is installed.

is my data safe?

We take it seriously and keep it simple:

  • Your account and garden are stored with Supabase, in the United States.
  • The app shows your photos only to you.
  • The AI providers we use do not train their models on your data.
  • We do not sell your data, and there are no ads or third party trackers in the app.

The details are in our privacy policy.

which AI do you use?
  • Plant identification and diagnosis: Kindwise Plant.id.
  • ask planter: Anthropic's Claude, with Google's Gemini as a backup if Claude is unavailable.

Nothing is sent to an AI service until you agree in the app, and you can turn it off any time in profile > ai & your data.

how do I leave the beta and delete my account?

In the app, go to profile > delete my account. If you already removed the app, use our delete account page and we will do it for you.

Uninstalling the app or TestFlight does not delete your account, so please delete it first if you want your data gone.

why did the app stop opening?

Beta builds expire after 90 days. Open TestFlight and install the latest build. Keeping automatic updates on in TestFlight avoids this.

questions? say hi.

Email hello@pocketplanter.app. A real person replies, usually within 2 business days. More help on the support page.
