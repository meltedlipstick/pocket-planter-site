quick fixes

common problems

Try these first. If nothing works, email us and we will sort it out together.

the app won't open, or TestFlight says the build expired

Beta builds expire after 90 days. Open TestFlight, tap pocket planter and install the latest build. Your plants are saved to your account, so nothing is lost.

To avoid this, turn on automatic updates for pocket planter in TestFlight.

I didn't get the confirmation or password reset email
  1. Wait a few minutes, then check your spam, junk and promotions folders.
  2. Search your inbox for "pocket planter".
  3. Make sure you typed the same email you signed up with.

Still nothing? Email us from that address and we will help.

care reminders don't show up
  1. On your iPhone, open Settings > Notifications > pocket planter and turn on Allow Notifications.
  2. Check that a Focus mode, like Sleep or Do Not Disturb, is not hiding them.
  3. Open pocket planter once so it can schedule the next reminders. They are set up on your phone.
care advice ignores my weather

pocket planter needs your approximate location to fetch the local forecast. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > pocket planter and choose While Using the App. Without it, the app still works, with general advice.

plant ID guessed the wrong plant
  • Use daylight and fill the frame with leaves from one plant.
  • Try a second photo from a different angle, or include a flower if it has one.

AI can be wrong, so treat the result as a suggestion. If it keeps missing, tell us the plant and we will look into it.

the light meter reading jumps around

Hold your iPhone still where the plant sits, with the camera facing the window, and give it a few seconds to settle. Readings change with clouds and time of day, so measure at the brightest part of the day for the best picture.

ask planter or plant ID says it's turned off

AI features only run after you agree to them. Go to profile > ai & your data to turn them on, or off again whenever you like.

I want my data, or want to delete my account
  • Export: profile > export my data.
  • Delete: profile > delete my account, or use the delete account page if you no longer have the app.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
